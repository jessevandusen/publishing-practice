Introducing Jesse VanDusen

Hi, I'm a Brooklyn-based freelance journalist who reports on food, community and the arts in New York City.

I'm also a fiction author.

In my spare time, you can find me biking or at the movie theater, or getting away from home to get lost in the woods or a new city.

Here are some of my favorite stories:

  1. An Epic Pilgrimage Across Three Great Religions : a travel story about religion and self discovery. (New York Times)
  2. Sam Altman May Control Our Future—Can He Be Trusted? : an anxiety-inducing look at the shifty ideology of OpenAI's founder, a nice pairing with You Can See Everything. (New Yorker)
  3. A Messy Evening with Bassvictim : the story of a trainwreck evening with a young band that's got an ear-bleeding sound and cool-kid following. (Pitchfork)
